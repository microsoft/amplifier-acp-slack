# Slack connector 0.11.1

A completed reply held by conversation limits now shows why it is waiting instead of remaining on “Sending the response…”. Status distinguishes waiting for a person, reply capacity, and a brief collaboration pause. Unknown delivery diagnostics use a generic waiting label; raw error content remains private.

This changes presentation only. Existing collaboration preferences, reply limits, model admission, and durable outbox behavior remain unchanged. Once a hold clears, the saved reply is delivered without rerunning the completed ACP turn, and its temporary indicator is removed.

Validation includes real connector processes with a loopback Slack service and an independent ACP fixture: complete a reply, hold it under the configured bot-exchange limit, expose the wait, release it through the configuration API, and verify one delivery with no repeated ACP prompt.
